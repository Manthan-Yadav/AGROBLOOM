Questions

Frequently Asked

Answers to the most common questions about our services.

Do I need to buy hardware for every service?

No. Services like Precision Farming and Farm Management run on your existing setup, while others like Drone Survey use our own equipment on a scheduled visit.

How long does onboarding take?

Most farms are fully onboarded within two to three weeks, including field survey and hardware installation.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time as your acreage or needs change.

Is support included in every plan?

All plans include email and chat support. Professional and Enterprise plans add priority phone support and a dedicated agronomist.
