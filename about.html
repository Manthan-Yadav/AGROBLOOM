Our Story

Nine years of building precision agriculture technology, one field at a time.

Our Mission

To give every grower, from smallholders to large operations, the same quality of data-driven decision-making once reserved for industrial agribusiness.

Our Vision

A world where farming decisions are guided by real-time data rather than guesswork — reducing waste, protecting soil, and feeding more people with less land.

What Drives Us

Core Values

The principles that shape every product decision we make.

Precision First

Every feature is built on verified field data, not assumptions.

Grower-Led

We build alongside farmers, testing every tool in real conditions.

Sustainability

Less water, fewer inputs, healthier soil — better outcomes for the land.

Transparency

Clear pricing, honest reporting, and data growers actually own.

Our Journey

Nine years in the field

From a single soil sensor prototype to a platform trusted across six continents.

2017

AgroBloom is founded

Started in a small research plot with a single soil-moisture prototype and three pilot farms.

2019

First 1,000 growers

Smart irrigation controllers shipped to over 1,000 farms across three countries.

2021

AI Analytics launches

Predictive yield modeling introduced, powered by satellite imagery and on-ground sensors.

2023

Drone survey fleet

SkyScout drones deployed for aerial multispectral mapping at scale.

2026

12,000+ growers strong

Now supporting farms across six continents and 40+ crop types.
