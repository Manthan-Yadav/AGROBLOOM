Questions

Frequently Asked

How long does installation take?

Most fields under 50 acres are fully sensor-equipped and live on the dashboard within a single day.

Does AgroBloom work without cell signal?

Yes — our devices use long-range LoRa mesh networking and store data locally until connectivity returns.

Can I integrate with my existing farm software?

AgroBloom ships with an open API and native integrations for the most common farm management platforms.

What crops is this suited for?

Our sensors and models are calibrated for row crops, orchards, vineyards, and greenhouse operations alike.

Is there a contract or can I cancel anytime?

Plans are month-to-month by default, with discounted annual terms available for larger operations.
